I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at George Mason University, where I build efficient systems for machine learning, with a focus on LLM inference and serving. My research explores GPU runtime scheduling, asynchronous I/O, memory management, and GPU kernel optimization for efficient execution across heterogeneous hardware. I work across the systems stack with tools including CUDA, Triton, TVM, PyTorch, SGLang, and vLLM, and my work has appeared at MobiCom, EuroSys, MobiSys, and SenSys.
