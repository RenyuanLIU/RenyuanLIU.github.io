I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at George Mason University, advised by [Prof. Shuochao Yao](https://yscacaca.github.io/). My research lies at the intersection of **AI infrastructure and ML systems**, with a focus on efficient **LLM inference and serving**. I am particularly interested in GPU runtimes, scheduling, memory and I/O systems, and systems support for large models on heterogeneous and resource-constrained hardware. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

Prior to George Mason, I received my M.S. in Automation and Signal from Institut d'Optique Théorique et Appliquée in Paris, France, and my B.E. in Optoelectronic Information Science and Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.
